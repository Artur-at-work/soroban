# Set and read numbers

Begin with one rod. To show 3, move three earth beads to the beam. To show 5, move the heaven bead to the beam. To show 7, combine the heaven bead and two earth beads.

{% include soroban.html rods=3 value=7 label="Set a number" %}

## Read from left to right

Each rod gives one digit from 0 to 9. On the board, the rightmost rod is ones, the next is tens, and the next is hundreds. Read the digits in that order. A rod with no beads against the beam is zero and still holds its place.

Try setting **204**: 2 on the hundreds rod, 0 on the tens rod, and 4 on the ones rod. Then clear the board and set **580**.

## Check yourself

- How do you show 8? Heaven bead plus three earth beads.
- What does an empty tens rod mean in 204? There are no tens; the hundreds and ones places stay put.
