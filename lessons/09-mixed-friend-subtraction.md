# Mixed friend subtraction

This is a review lesson for choosing between direct, 5-friend, and 10-friend moves. For subtractions from 6 through 9, a 10-friend sequence is often useful when the current rod does not have enough value.

## Example: 13 − 6

The ones rod shows 3, so subtracting 6 directly is not possible. Use **−10 +4**: remove one ten (13 becomes 3), then add 4 on the ones rod (3 becomes 7). The answer is 7.

{% include soroban.html rods=3 value=7 label="See 7 on the board" %}

Always check that the intermediate move is possible. The final arithmetic is the same, but the friend sequence depends on the beads currently showing.

## Practice

Try 12 − 6, 15 − 8, 23 − 7, and 41 − 9. Explain why each move is available.
