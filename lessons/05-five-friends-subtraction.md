# 5-friends subtraction

When a rod does not have enough active earth beads to subtract, subtract 5 and add back the amount that completes the pair.

| Subtract | Move |
|---|---|
| −1 | −5 + 4 |
| −2 | −5 + 3 |
| −3 | −5 + 2 |
| −4 | −5 + 1 |

{% include soroban.html rods=2 value=6 label="Try 6 − 2" %}

## Example: 6 − 2

Six is showing as 5 + 1. There are not two earth beads to move down. For −2:

1. Subtract 5 by moving the heaven bead away: 6 becomes 1.
2. Add the friend of 2, which is 3: 1 becomes 4.

The result is 4. Keep the order: subtract 5, then add the friend.

## Practice

Try 5 − 1, 6 − 3, 7 − 4, and 16 − 2.
