# 5-friends addition

The 5-friends pairs make 5: **1 and 4**, **2 and 3**, **3 and 2**, **4 and 1**. When there are not enough earth beads for a direct addition, add 5 and take away the amount that completes the pair.

| Add | Move |
|---|---|
| +1 | +5 − 4 |
| +2 | +5 − 3 |
| +3 | +5 − 2 |
| +4 | +5 − 1 |

## Example: 2 + 4

Two is showing. Four earth beads are not free to move up, so use the pair for 4:

1. Add 5 by moving the heaven bead toward the beam: 2 becomes 7.
2. Subtract 1 by moving one earth bead away: 7 becomes 6.

The result is 6. The small move is the **friend** of the amount you want to add.

## Practice

Try 1 + 4, 2 + 3, 3 + 4, and 14 + 2. Explain the two moves aloud.
