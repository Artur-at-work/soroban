# Meet the soroban

The soroban is a place-value abacus. Each vertical rod is one digit. The rod nearest the unit dot is the ones place; moving left gives tens, hundreds, and so on.

## The beads

Each rod has one upper, or **heaven**, bead worth 5 and four lower, or **earth**, beads worth 1 each. Beads count only when they touch the beam in the middle. Move the beads on the board below and watch the readout.

## A relaxed starting position

Sit comfortably with the soroban level and close enough that your shoulders can relax. Keep the frame still. Use your thumb to move earth beads up toward the beam and your index finger to move them down. Use your index finger for the heaven bead in either direction. Reset unused beads away from the beam before reading a number.

For now, practice one clean move at a time. Accuracy and a light touch matter more than speed.

## Check yourself

- Which bead is worth 5? The single bead above the beam.
- When does a bead count? When it rests against the beam.
- Where is the ones rod? The rod with the unit dot.
