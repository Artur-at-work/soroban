# Direct subtraction

Subtract directly when enough active earth beads are on the rod. Move one earth bead down, away from the beam, for −1.

{% include soroban.html rods=2 value=8 label="Try 8 − 3" %}

## Example: 8 − 3

1. Set 8: the heaven bead and three earth beads are active.
2. Move three earth beads down and away from the beam.
3. The heaven bead and no earth beads remain: 5.

You can directly subtract up to the number of active earth beads. If there are not enough, use a friend method instead of forcing the move.

## Practice

Try 4 − 2, 9 − 3, 7 − 1, and 27 − 2.
