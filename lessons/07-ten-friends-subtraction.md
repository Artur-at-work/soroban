# 10-friends subtraction

When the current rod does not have enough value to subtract, subtract 1 from the rod on its left and add the 10-friend to the current rod.

## Example: 32 − 8

The friend of 8 is 2. Subtract 1 ten from the tens rod (3 becomes 2), then add 2 on the ones rod (2 becomes 4). The result is 24.

{% include soroban.html rods=3 value=24 label="See 24 on the board" %}

The rule for −n is **−1 on the rod to the left, +(10 − n) on this rod**. Make sure a value is available to subtract from the left-hand rod.

## Practice

Try 14 − 8, 25 − 7, 42 − 9, and 63 − 5. Name the friend before moving beads.
