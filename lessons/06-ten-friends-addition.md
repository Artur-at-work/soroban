# 10-friends addition

The 10-friends pairs make 10: **1 and 9**, **2 and 8**, **3 and 7**, **4 and 6**, and **5 and 5**. When the ones rod cannot hold the full addition, add the amount by subtracting its friend from the ones rod and adding 1 to the tens rod.

## Example: 8 + 5

The friend of 5 is 5. On the ones rod, subtract 5 from 8 to leave 3; on the tens rod, add 1. The rods now show 13.

The rule for +n is **−(10 − n) on this rod, +1 on the rod to its left**. You need enough value on the current rod to subtract that friend.

## Practice

Try 7 + 5, 6 + 8, 4 + 9, and 28 + 5. Say the friend and the carry before moving beads.
