# Curriculum

Work through the lessons in order. Get comfortable with each bead move before moving on; speed comes later.

1. [Meet the soroban]({{ '/lessons/00-meet-the-soroban' | relative_url }})
2. [Set and read numbers]({{ '/lessons/01-set-and-read' | relative_url }})
3. [Direct addition]({{ '/lessons/02-direct-addition' | relative_url }})
4. [Direct subtraction]({{ '/lessons/03-direct-subtraction' | relative_url }})
5. [5-friends addition]({{ '/lessons/04-five-friends-addition' | relative_url }})
6. [5-friends subtraction]({{ '/lessons/05-five-friends-subtraction' | relative_url }})
7. [10-friends addition]({{ '/lessons/06-ten-friends-addition' | relative_url }})
8. [10-friends subtraction]({{ '/lessons/07-ten-friends-subtraction' | relative_url }})
9. [Mixed friend addition]({{ '/lessons/08-mixed-friend-addition' | relative_url }})
10. [Mixed friend subtraction]({{ '/lessons/09-mixed-friend-subtraction' | relative_url }})

Later units will extend this path through longer calculations, multiplication, division, decimals, roots, and anzan.
